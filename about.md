This site gathers the current materials, references, and event details for the community conversation around next-generation ecosystems in scientific computing.

## What This Site Covers

- Recent workshops and Birds-of-a-Feather sessions
- Reports and references that motivate the discussion
- Working materials for the SC26 BOF and related community activities

## Why It Exists

Scientific computing is being reshaped by AI, heterogeneous computing, and evolving software practices. This site is a lightweight public home for convening material, background references, and event details that support that transition.
